🏗️ Kaboom Crane

Tap in rhythm, then let it fly

A wrecking ball hangs from a crane, and you build its swing exactly the way you'd push someone on a playground swing — tap in time with the arc and the amplitude climbs. When it's big enough, hit RELEASE and the ball flies off tangentially into the building.

Why it works: Everyone already knows how to push a swing, so there is no tutorial — and the payoff is a physics demolition with slow-motion, collapsing towers and chained TNT. Two dopamine spikes per loop instead of one.

▶ PLAY NOW Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Tap in rhythm with the swing to build amplitude — aim for PERFECT PUSH.
  2. 2When the meter is ready, hit RELEASE to launch the ball.
  3. 3Wreck the target percentage of the building before you run out of balls.

What's in it

  • Angular pendulum physics with genuine timing windows
  • Perfect-push streaks that ladder up in pitch
  • Glass shatters, wood splinters, brick cracks then breaks
  • Unsupported blocks collapse, and falling blocks crush what is below
  • TNT with radius damage and fused chain reactions
  • Freeze-frame, white flash and 0.35x slow-motion on first impact

The meta layer

Contracts with silly names fund four upgrade tracks — Heavier Ball, Chain Length, Extra Ball and TNT Luck — each visibly changing the demolition.
