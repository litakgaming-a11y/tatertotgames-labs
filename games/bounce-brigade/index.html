Bounce Brigade

Drop a trampoline, launch a rescue

Citizens are leaping from a smoking building at their own pace, and you are the fire brigade. Drag to place trampolines anywhere along their fall, tilting each one toward your drag direction, and chain the bounces across rooftops and awnings into the ambulance net.

Play now → Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Drag to place a trampoline; the drag direction sets its angle.
  2. 2Jumpers bounce automatically, chain them across the skyline.
  3. 3Deliver enough citizens to the net before you run out of hearts.

What's in it

  • Deterministic bounce response so every plan is repeatable
  • Squash and stretch with a pitch-laddering boing on each hop
  • Style points for flips mid-arc
  • Wind gusts, moving nets and bonus hoops
  • Cats, which are considerably floatier than people
  • Comedic misses, a dropped jumper lands in a bush

The meta layer

Coins buy trampoline skins and station upgrades: a wider net, a third trampoline, and a slow-motion charge.
