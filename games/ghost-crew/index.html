👻 Ghost Crew

Team up with your past self

Some doors need two people, and you are alone. So you run the level, tap REWIND, and your previous run replays as a translucent ghost — standing on the plate it stood on, holding the gate it held open — while you play the next member of the crew through the door it just opened.

Why it works: It is the most design-forward idea in the collection: the puzzle is choreographing yourself, and the rewind is a full-screen VHS scrub that makes every attempt feel like a rewind on tape.

▶ PLAY NOW Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Hold the left or right half of the screen to move; ledges are auto-jumped.
  2. 2Press REWIND when you have done your part — your run replays as a ghost.
  3. 3Get every crew member into the exit door at the same time.

What's in it

  • Ghosts replay recorded positions exactly — verified at zero deviation
  • Ten hand-authored single-screen levels plus mirrored variants
  • Timed plates, AND-gates, one-use levers and moving-platform lifts
  • Full-screen VHS scrub rewind with genuinely reversed audio
  • A TEAMWORK burst when every self reaches the door together
  • Recording capped at 60 seconds so memory cannot grow unbounded

The meta layer

Stars earned by finishing under par unlock six character skins and a Crew Photo wall — one silly framed group shot per world of ten levels.
