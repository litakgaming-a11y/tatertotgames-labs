Signal Box

Pull the levers, run the railway

A railway signal box, with an enamel track diagram on the wall and a lever frame on the floor. Every station is a puzzle: a diagram, a row of levers and a timetable. Set the points, clear the signals, and bring every train to its platform without two of them meeting. The trains run on a clock you can start, stop and step one minute at a time.

Play now → Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Tap a lever to pull it, and tap it again to put it back. Red levers work signals, black levers work points, blue levers set which way a single line runs.
  2. 2Start the clock and watch the trains run. Stop it whenever you like, or step it on one minute at a time.
  3. 3Bring every train in. Fewer lever pulls earn more stars: three stars at par.

What's in it

  • Sixty stations across five lines, every one checked solvable, each with a par worked out by a solver
  • The Branch: points and signals, with nothing to stop a collision but you
  • The Frame: an interlocked lever frame that locks the dangerous moves and asks for more pulls in return
  • The Single Line: direction levers, passing loops and trains that must cross on time
  • The Yard: slow goods trains, sidings, headshunts and trains that reverse
  • The Timetable: the boxes you know, at rush hour

The meta layer

Each line opens when you have worked eight stations on the one before. Stations keep your best count of pulls, and the stars add up in the register.
