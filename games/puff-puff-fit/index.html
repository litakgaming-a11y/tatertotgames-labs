Puff Puff Fit

Hold to inflate, release before the pop

A squishy blob sits in a chamber ringed with spikes. Hold anywhere and it inflates; let go and it locks in place. Fill enough of the chamber to clear the round, but every extra millimetre of greed brings the blob closer to a spike, and a pop costs a heart.

Play now → Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Press and hold anywhere to start inflating the blob.
  2. 2Watch the fill meter and the spikes, the blob's eyes widen as it gets close.
  3. 3Release before you touch a spike. Fill 92%+ for a PERFECT and triple coins.
