✂️ Buzzcut Buddies

Buzz the fuzz, reveal the buddy

A creature arrives buried under hundreds of individual fuzz strands. Drag to run the clippers over it and each strand clips away with a crunchy tick, the pitch shifting with how fast you sweep — until the buddy underneath is revealed, accessories and all.

Why it works: The satisfying-reveal genre reliably produces the cheapest installs in mobile, and there is no fail state here at all: the session is pure texture, collection and payoff.

▶ PLAY NOW Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Drag anywhere to run the clippers across the fuzz.
  2. 2Respect the dashed keep-zones — "leave the mohawk!" means it.
  3. 3Reach 95% trimmed to reveal your buddy and send it to the salon.

What's in it

  • Up to 900 individual strands, spatially hashed so it holds 60fps
  • Crunch ticks whose pitch and density follow your stroke speed
  • A continuous clipper hum that pitch-shifts as you sweep
  • Hidden ticklish spots that trigger a giggle and a wiggle
  • Golden strands, hidden accessories and a FLOW combo up to 5x
  • Double-layer coats from level 6: shaggy outer, dense undercoat

The meta layer

Every groomed buddy joins your salon with a procedurally generated name, and the salon gains decor every 10 clients. A VIP client pays double after a four-hour gap.
