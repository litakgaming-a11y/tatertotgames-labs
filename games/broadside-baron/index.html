Broadside Baron

Fire at the top of the swell

Your galleon rides a living swell, and the roll of the hull physically aims your cannons. Tap and every gun fires in sequence like a drumroll, each shot leaving at its own elevation, so a volley loosed at the crest arcs across the water and one fired in the trough ploughs straight into it.

Play now → Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Watch the swell, your hull rolls with it and your guns follow.
  2. 2Tap to fire a broadside; hit the crest for a PERFECT VOLLEY at double damage.
  3. 3Hold to brace against rough seas, swipe to change range and dodge their guns.

What's in it

  • The ocean swell is the metronome, physical, visible, no timing bar
  • Each cannon fires at its own elevation, so volleys genuinely spread
  • Positional damage: waterline hits flood, mast hits drop sails
  • Roguelite voyages across branching maps of battles, merchants and storms
  • Stacking crew-card drafts, Powder Monkey, Bosun, Chain Shot, Kraken's Luck
  • Fog banks where you fire on the sound of gunports alone

The meta layer

Plunder survives every run and rebuilds your home Port across twelve stages, docks, tavern, shipwright, lighthouse, alongside permanent upgrades to hull, powder quality, crew quarters and spyglass, plus ship skins earned by voyage milestones.
