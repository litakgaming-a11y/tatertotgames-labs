⚡ Volt Rush

Slide under the strike before it lands

A storm rolls over the city and every strike telegraphs itself: a reticle charges on the rail for about a second before the bolt falls. Drag your rod cart underneath in time and the lightning bends into the rod, banking volts and building a combo that caps at eight.

Why it works: It pairs a tight one-second skill loop with an idle city that keeps earning while you're gone — the arcade layer holds the session, the idle layer brings you back tomorrow.

▶ PLAY NOW Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Drag anywhere to slide the rod cart along the rail.
  2. 2Get under the charging reticle before the bolt drops.
  3. 3Chain catches for combo multipliers, and grab golden bolts for 5x.

What's in it

  • Jagged multi-segment bolts with branch forks and afterimage flicker
  • Combo-scaled screen shake and white flash frames on every catch
  • Double and triple simultaneous strikes, plus fake-out reticles that fizzle
  • Golden bolts worth 5x with their own fanfare
  • Fourteen city buildings that light up as you bank volts
  • Offline earnings capped at 8 hours with a welcome-back claim

The meta layer

Volts light a fourteen-building skyline; lit buildings generate coins per second even while you are away, funding Wider Rod, Volt Value, Storm Frequency and Battery upgrades.
