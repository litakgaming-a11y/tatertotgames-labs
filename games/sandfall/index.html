🏜️ Sandfall

Draw a ledge — the sand obeys

Colored sand pours from spouts at the top of the screen, and every line you draw becomes a shelf it piles onto, slides down, and streams off. Route each stream into its matching jar — but your shelves erode under the flow, your ink is limited, and a stray stream contaminates the wrong jar with an ugly brown swirl.

Why it works: The simulation is the ad: piles forming true slopes and streams forking around obstacles need zero explanation. And because shelves erode, a solution never stays solved — mastery is continuous re-routing, which gives it the highest replay density of the physics wave.

▶ PLAY NOW Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Drag to draw shelf lines — your ink meter is the budget.
  2. 2Route each colored stream into its matching jar; shelves erode as sand grinds over them.
  3. 3Fill every quota. Fans, acid pools and moving jars arrive as you climb.

What's in it

  • Real cellular-automata falling sand with true angle-of-repose piles
  • Jars fill in visible strata — the layers you poured stay layered
  • Drawn shelves erode grain by grain, so every fix is temporary
  • Contamination is recoverable but costs quota — a brown swirl marks the shame
  • Fans that blow falling grains and acid pools that dissolve them
  • Settled sand sleeps, keeping the whole simulation at 60fps on phones

The meta layer

Completed jars keep their actual poured strata pattern and join a Terrarium Shelf — no two jars are alike. Material unlocks (glow, heavy, galaxy sand) and upgrades: Ink+, Slow Erosion, Wide Jars, Contamination Filter.
