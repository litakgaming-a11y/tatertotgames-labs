🧊 Freeze Frame!

Tap the water, freeze the bridge

A queue of round little critters waddles right, toward the exit door — and the ground between them is open water. Tap a gap and it flash-freezes into an ice bridge that lasts a couple of seconds before cracking apart, so you're always juggling which gap to freeze next.

Why it works: Cute things in mild peril is one of the most durable hooks in casual games, and the visible melt timer turns every crossing into a small, readable panic you can actually manage.

▶ PLAY NOW Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Tap any water gap to freeze an ice bridge across it.
  2. 2The ring shows the melt timer — the ice cracks, then shatters.
  3. 3Get enough critters to the door. There is a short cooldown before you can refreeze.

What's in it

  • Independent melt timers and cooldowns per gap
  • Cracks that propagate in two audible stages before the shatter
  • Jumping fish that bonk critters backwards, and geysers that make them queue
  • Red-beanie "zoomer" critters that move at 1.75x speed
  • A rising two-note chirp ladder for consecutive rescues
  • Tuned so the quota always leaves two spare critters

The meta layer

Every critter you save moves into a Snow Village on the title screen, which levels up every 15 rescues — igloo, campfire, snowman, string lights, ice statue, sled, aurora.
