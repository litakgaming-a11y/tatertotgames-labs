Beam Team

Tap a mirror, the light bends

A lighthouse throws one continuous golden beam across a dark grid. Every mirror you tap rotates 45 degrees with a clockwork click, and the beam instantly whips to its new path. Route it through prisms and colour filters until every lantern is lit and the level blooms out of the dark into full colour.

Play now → Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

  • Fireflies that gather along lit beam segments
  • Rotating obstacle fans, mirror budgets and dual-lighthouse levels
  • Every level solvable, verified by search across levels 1–30
