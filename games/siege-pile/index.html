⚔️ Siege Pile

The pile becomes the ladder

Drag back the catapult and fling a knight at the castle wall. The ones who clear it capture ground. The ones who fall short land in a clattering heap, and that heap is solid. It piles, it settles, and then the knights inside it stand up and brace, turning themselves into a ramp for everyone who comes after.

▶ PLAY NOW Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Drag back and release to launch a knight from the catapult.
  2. 2Tap mid-flight to tuck, less drag, and a higher bounce off the pile.
  3. 3Short throws heap up and stand as a ramp. Use them to reach the top.

What's in it

  • Articulated ragdoll knights that tumble, clang and lose their helmets
  • Fallen knights pile into solid, climbable terrain
  • Piled knights stand and brace, organising the heap into a ramp
  • Defenders shove your piles over; moats swallow throws with no pile at all
  • Knight types: Heavy, Tumbler, wall-sticking Grappler, Standard-Bearer
  • Keeps with portcullises that need three knights on a plate at once

The meta layer

Your Warband is a roster drafted between sieges, each knight type changing how piles build. Four upgrade tracks tune catapult tension, tuck window, knight count and pile stability, while captured castles fill in a conquest map.
