A Kinfold economy probe. Capture charms are the game's real scarcity — and today they come from tapping a recipe.

Here you feed cores in one at a time. The pot tempers the whole batch: every core becomes a charm at whatever tier you reach, so a hotter pot is worth more per core — and past the line, every core you add might crack it.

You can never lose what you put in — a crack banks the batch at the last tier you passed. The only thing at stake is how much you leave on the table.
