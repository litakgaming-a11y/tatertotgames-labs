🔥 Crucible

Stop before it cracks

Feed cores into the pot one at a time. Each one raises the tier and raises the heat, and the pot tempers the whole batch, every core becomes a charm at whatever tier you reach. Past the line, each core you add might crack it. Bank early and you leave value on the table; push too far and the crack banks the last tier you passed.

▶ PLAY NOW Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1FEED a core. The tier climbs, and so does the heat.
  2. 2Watch the line. Below it a feed can never crack; above it the crack chance is shown and rising.
  3. 3BANK to keep the whole batch at its current tier.
  4. 4If it cracks you still keep the batch at the last tier you passed. Nothing is ever confiscated.
