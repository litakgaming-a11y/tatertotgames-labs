Ping Pilot

The cave is dark. Your tap is sonar.

You are piloting a salvage sub through a cave with the lights off. Tapping fires a sonar ping, an expanding ring that paints the walls, the pearls and the hazards for about two seconds before they fade back into black. Pings are limited, and they recharge slowly.

Play now → Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Hold to steer the sub toward your finger.
  2. 2Tap to fire a sonar ping and reveal the cave around you.
  3. 3Collect the pearl quota and reach the exit, quietly.

What's in it

  • Expanding wavefront that lights objects in sequence as it passes
  • Echo audio delay that scales with distance
  • An anglerfish that hunts your pings, information has a cost
  • Current jets, false-echo crystals and one-ping blackout levels
  • A faint ambient outline nearby so darkness never feels unfair
  • High-contrast palette that stays readable on a dim phone screen

The meta layer

Pearls fund the Reef Vault: ping radius, ping count, a silent thruster and a pearl magnet.
