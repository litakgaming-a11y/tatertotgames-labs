🛰️ Slingshot Salvage

One drag, and gravity does the rest

Drag back from your docked pod and a dotted trajectory bends live around every planet's gravity well. Release, and the pod flies the arc you plotted — hoovering up space junk, stealing speed from close fly-bys, and crashing spectacularly if you misjudge a planet.

Why it works: The aim-assist arc is the hook: it teaches orbital intuition in about three seconds without a word of tutorial, and it makes a good bank shot feel earned rather than lucky.

▶ PLAY NOW Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Drag back from the pod to aim — the dotted arc previews your flight.
  2. 2Release to launch. Skim close to a planet for a GRAVITY ASSIST bonus.
  3. 3Collect the junk quota before you run out of launches.

What's in it

  • Trajectory preview that runs the exact same physics as the flight
  • Inverse-square gravity, clamped near surfaces so it stays forgiving
  • Magnet-suck collection with an ascending pentatonic combo ladder
  • Orbiting planets from level 4 and comet hazards from level 6
  • Gravity-assist detection with pitch-bend whoosh on fast fly-bys
  • Four upgrade tracks that visibly change how the pod flies

The meta layer

Scrap funds a garage with four five-level tracks: Magnet Radius, Launch Power, Extra Launch and Lucky Star — each one changing the physics you play with.
