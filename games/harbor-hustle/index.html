Harbor Hustle

Tap to stop, tap to go

Ferries, tankers and jet-skis all cross the same harbour on fixed routes toward their colour-matched piers. You cannot steer any of them, you can only tap a boat to hold it, and tap again to send it on. Six hulls, one brain, no collisions.

Play now → Runs in your browser · no install · works on phones

How to play

  1. 1Tap a boat to hold it in place; tap again to send it on.
  2. 2Time the crossings so nobody meets in the middle.
  3. 3Squeeze past for a CLOSE CALL bonus, three collisions ends the shift.

What's in it

  • Nearest-boat-within-radius tapping, so a slightly-off thumb still works
  • Close-call bonuses that reward brave play
  • Jet-skis that dart, tankers that accelerate slowly
  • Fog banks, a timed drawbridge, and an impatient VIP yacht worth 3x
  • Boats queue at the closed bridge rather than crashing into it
  • Balance-tested across 20 shifts with no difficulty cliffs

The meta layer

Earnings build a harbour town along the shore, lighthouse, market, ferris wheel, visible from the play field itself, plus strike and patience upgrades.
