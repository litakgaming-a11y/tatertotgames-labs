# Surface brief: Pinball Almanac (games/pinball-almanac/play.html)

Mode: Experience. The table is the whole screen; menus are flyer pages between games.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An old printed pinball flyer come to life, not a neon arcade cabinet. The playfield is screen-printed: a deep navy ground with two flat inks per table, laid in halftone and printed slightly off register. Scores roll on reels like an electro-mechanical machine and chime as they count. It refuses the pinball default of a glowing dot-matrix display, chrome gradients everywhere, lens flares and synthwave purple.

OWN-WORLD: Navy ink ground (#1d2340), aged flyer stock (#efe6d2) for every menu page, and two inks per table: vermilion and marigold for County Fair, teal and marigold for Tidewater, vermilion and teal for Night Train, pink and teal for Comet Year. Lamps are designed lit and unlit: an unlit insert is a dull, desaturated version of its colour, a lit one is the full ink with white lettering. A condensed bold face (Bahnschrift, Roboto Condensed or Arial Narrow, whichever the device has) for all lettering.

STORY: The player opens the almanac, picks a season's table, fits the parts they have earned into its sockets, and plays. Each table has its own word to spell, a drop bank that lights the lock, three modes started from the saucer, multiball with a growing jackpot, and a wizard mode for finishing all three. Every table carries six missions, and stars from them unlock the next season and new parts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The County Fair table plays itself behind the title in attract mode; the backglass strip above it rolls the score on reels. The title reads down the flyer with one vermilion button.

FORM: Pinned by the user's choice of the Pinball Almanac concept (an old printed pinball flyer and a screen-printed playfield), which beats the roll. Seed key f98adeab, assigned index 7 not built because of the pin. Raises kept from the dealt challengers: WPA park poster (each table is a landscape flattened into stacked flat planes of ink, and the season's name is set in blocky caps), public theater street poster (the flyer headline is cropped by the page edge and set on a slight diagonal), night instrument six-pack (the backglass readouts are few and legible at a glance: score, ball, bonus, one message line), hand-drawn zine (the almanac explains each mission in one plain sentence, the way a friend would).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
