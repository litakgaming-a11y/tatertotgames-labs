# Surface brief: Fire Season (games/fire-season/play.html)

Mode: Operate inside an Experience. The player commands on a map; legibility of state beats atmosphere.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A ranger's working map, not a war-game HUD. The ground is a survey-style contour map with fuel tints, and every order is drawn on it like pencil on paper. It refuses the strategy default of a dark sci-fi overlay, glowing unit icons and a minimap in the corner.

OWN-WORLD: Cool map paper (#edf0e8), straw grass, sage brush, woodland green timber with tree dots, stippled rock, blue water with an inked shoreline, brown contour lines with every fifth heavier, a red road cased in black, black square houses. One signal orange (#e2521c on the map, #c2410c for buttons) for fire, selection and the primary action; retardant pink for air drops. Forms are paper panels ruled in ink with a workhorse sans and tabular numbers.

STORY: The player reads where the wind will push the fire, draws line ahead of it, protects the homes, and learns to beat the afternoon burn period; between calls they build a station and a crew that gets better every season.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The contour map fills the map area under a forecast strip of hourly wind arrows; the deck (bottom on portrait, right on landscape) shows the clock, time controls, acres, homes and containment, the radio line, the crew list and the air tanker.

FORM: Pinned by the user's choice of the Fire Season concept (a ranger's contour map, forecast strip, one signal orange, clipboard roster), which beats the roll. Seed key ee269146, assigned index 4 not built because of the pin. Raises kept from the dealt challengers: ink basin (the newest burn is darkest and ages to ash, so age reads as value), phosphor terminal (events print themselves on a radio line instead of popping up), tensegrity column (every edge of the fire reads as held in black or open in red), deep dive (the day runs through named states: Morning, Burn period, Laying down).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
