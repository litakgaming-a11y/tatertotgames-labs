# Surface brief: Clockwork Sky (games/clockwork-sky/play.html)

Mode: Experience. The player is inside the instrument; the orrery leads from the first viewport.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A working astronomical instrument, not a game HUD. The sky is a graduated brass limb with engraved rings, and every control is a part of the mechanism (a winding key, a mainspring gauge, gear dials, a forecast track). It refuses the idle-game default of chunky candy buttons, glowing currency counters and a shop list over a cartoon backdrop.

OWN-WORLD: Prussian night ink ground (#0c1322), fine cream engraving lines at low alpha, one brass accent (#d8a94e / #f0c874) for everything mechanical, vermilion (#e2573c) only for line-ups of four or more, verdigris (#74b8a8) for ice and moons. Planets are hand-tinted engravings: flat watercolor tints with hatch shading on the side away from the star. Roman plate lettering in a serif stack with wide tracking. Sharp 2px corners; pressing a control inverts it to brass; unaffordable is hatched.

STORY: The player winds the sky, watches planets pay as they come round, learns that planets on gears that fit together line up and pay more, zeroes the sky to bring them into step, and eventually lets the star collapse to light a stranger one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title plate in wide-tracked roman caps over the live orrery, one brass "Wind the sky" plate, tatertotgames.com underneath. In play: the orrery fills the sky region with light and rate top-left and mute top-right; the deck (bottom on portrait, right on landscape) opens with the one-minute line-up track, then Planets, Gears, Star chart and Logbook.

FORM: Pinned by the user's choice of the Clockwork Sky concept (a live astronomical instrument in fine ink lines, one brass accent, dials instead of buttons), which beats the roll. Seed key ce25b910, assigned index 6 (a mission-control orbital plot) not built because of the pin. Raises kept from the dealt challengers: drum machine step row (a chase track that always shows where "now" is, motion locked to the mechanism's clock), one-bit desktop (strict control-state grammar: pressing inverts, disabled is hatched), passport (a logbook where observations accumulate across every star), orizuru sequence (collapse keeps the chart and logbook, so every earlier star leaves its mark).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
