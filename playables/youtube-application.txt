YouTube Playables application: TaterTot Games


1. Describe your game(s).

We're TaterTot Games (https://tatertotgames.com). We make small games you can pick up in a few seconds and keep coming back to.

Each game is built around one simple action. You hold to inflate a blob without popping it on a spike, draw a line of dominoes and watch it fall, or drop crates onto a boat that really floats and starts to tip. Behind that there's something to grow: coins, upgrades, a town, a garden or a collection.

They're plain HTML5 with no engine. Every game is one small file (under 130 KB), loads almost instantly and works with touch or mouse in portrait, landscape and on desktop. Our YouTube builds already use the Playables SDK for cloud saves, pause and resume, YouTube's mute, and the loading events. They make no outside network calls.
